How the sim works

Every game is played possession by possession, ${N} times. Each player gets his real shooting, turnovers, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, fouls drawn and minutes from his ESPN game logs, using only games before the game date. For games already played, each team's roster is its active list from that night, which teams publish before tip-off, so injured players never play in the sim. For upcoming games, it's whoever played the team's last 3 games, minus anyone on today's injury report. Team pace, defense, rebounding, turnovers and how each team defends guards, forwards and centers come from its last 10 box scores. Back-to-backs, rest days, miles traveled and altitude adjust each team's shooting, and today's injury report sits injured players in upcoming games. Each player's on/off rating, from every play-by-play of the season and updated every night, nudges the shooting of whoever is on the floor, possession by possession. Every dial is in Settings. The team that wins more of the simulated games is the pick, and the records above count how often that pick won for real. Picks are saved in this browser, so each game is only simmed once. "Watch the sim" adds real shot locations and passing from ESPN play-by-play for the animation.
